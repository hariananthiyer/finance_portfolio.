Use company annual reports, exchange filings, investor presentations, and reputable industry sources. Record source links and dates.

02 · Analyse

Show your assumptions, calculations, key drivers, peer selection logic, and risks. Separate reported data from your own estimates.

03 · Communicate

Summarise the question, method, findings, limitations, and conclusion in a clean PDF or spreadsheet that another person can follow.
